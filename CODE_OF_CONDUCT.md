# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Maestro a respectful, inclusive
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas, code and decisions.
- Give feedback about the work, never the person.
- Protect private information and report security concerns through the private
  channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
doxxing and deliberate disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[destek@escompanystudio.com](mailto:destek@escompanystudio.com). Maintainers
will review reports confidentially and may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban an individual when
necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions and other spaces where someone represents the project.
